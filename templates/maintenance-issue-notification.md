# Template: Maintenance Issue Notification

**Use when:** you need to proactively tell a guest about a maintenance issue — that a technician is on the way, that an issue has been identified, or that it has been resolved. This is different from the apology template: use this for status updates, and pair it with an apology only if the inconvenience was significant.

**Tips:** Be specific about the issue and the room/area. Give a realistic time, not an optimistic guess. Always close the loop once the issue is actually resolved, not just visited.

---

**Subject:** Update on the maintenance issue in your room, [Guest name]

Dear [Guest name],

Thank you for letting us know about [short description of the issue] in [room number / area]. Here is where things stand:

- **Status:** [Technician on the way / Issue identified, parts needed / Resolved]
- **Expected time:** [time estimate, or "resolved as of (time)"]
- **What we're doing:** [brief description, for example "Our maintenance team is replacing the unit's thermostat."]

[If applicable: "In the meantime, we've moved you to room [number], ready for you now."]

[If applicable: "As a gesture of goodwill for the inconvenience, we've added [compensation] to your stay."]

If you notice the issue is still not resolved after this, please reply to this email or call the front desk at 555-0100 (fake number) and we will follow up right away.

Thank you for your patience.

Warm regards,
[Your name]
