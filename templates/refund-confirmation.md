# Template: Confirmation of a Refund

**Use when:** a refund has been approved and submitted, and you need to confirm the details in writing.

**Tips:** Double-check the amount, the booking number, and the payment method before sending. Always include the timeframe so the guest knows what to expect.

---

**Subject:** Your Pahuway refund for booking [confirmation number]

Dear [Guest name],

We are writing to confirm that your refund has been approved. Here are the details:

- **Booking number:** [confirmation number]
- **Stay dates:** [arrival date] to [departure date]
- **Refund amount:** PHP [amount]
- **Refunded to:** the card or account ending in [last 4 digits]
- **Expected timing:** 5 to 7 business days from today ([date])

The time it takes for the money to show in your account can depend on your bank. If you have not seen it after 7 business days, please reply to this email with your booking number and we will check it for you.

We are sorry we could not welcome you this time and we hope to see you at Pahuway in the future.

Warm regards,
[Your name]
