# Template: Follow-up After a Resolved Complaint

**Use when:** a complaint has been resolved and you want to check that the guest is satisfied. Send it within one day of the resolution, or on the guest's last day.

**Tips:** Mention the specific issue so the guest knows this is not a form message. Keep it short. Invite a reply, but do not ask them to fill out a survey.

---

**Subject:** Checking in after [short description of the issue]

Dear [Guest name],

I am following up on [short description of the issue, for example: "the noise you reported on the night of 14 August"]. I hope the steps we took, [what was done], have made a difference for you.

Is everything now as it should be? If anything is still not right, please tell me and I will look into it personally. You can reply to this email or call the front desk on 555-0100 (fake number).

Thank you again for letting us know. Your feedback helps us improve Pahuway for every guest.

Warm regards,
[Your name]
Guest Support, Pahuway
