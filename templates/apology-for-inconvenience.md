# Template: Apology for an Inconvenience

**Use when:** a guest had a problem (a delay, a broken item, a mistake) and you want to apologize and explain what happens next.

**Tips:** Be specific about what went wrong. Say what you are doing about it. Do not blame the guest or other staff. Only offer what you are allowed to offer.

---

**Subject:** Our apologies, [Guest name]

Dear [Guest name],

Thank you for telling us about [short description of the problem]. I am sorry for the inconvenience this caused during your stay at Pahuway. This is not the experience we want for our guests.

Here is what we have done:

- [Action taken, for example: "We have fixed the air conditioning in room 412."]
- [Second action, if any, for example: "We have added a PHP 300 drink voucher to your room."]

If there is anything else we can do, please reply to this email or call the front desk on 555-0100 (fake number).

Thank you for your patience and understanding.

Warm regards,
[Your name]
Guest Support, Pahuway
support@example.com
