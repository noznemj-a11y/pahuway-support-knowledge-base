# Lost and Found Items

## Purpose

This article explains how to handle items that guests leave behind at Pahuway, and how to help guests who have lost something. It keeps belongings safe, records them clearly, and returns them to the right person.

## When to use this article

- A guest reports that they lost or forgot an item in their room, the lobby, the pool area, or the restaurant.
- Housekeeping or another staff member finds an item that no one has claimed.
- A guest has already checked out and asks the hotel to look for or send back an item.
- Someone asks to collect an item on behalf of a guest.

## Steps

1. Ask the guest for their name, room number and stay dates, a description of the item (color, brand, size, and anything unique about it), and where and when they think they lost it.
2. Search the lost and found shelf and the found-items log for a match. Also ask housekeeping and the restaurant team to check their areas.
3. If an item is found by staff, record it in the found-items log right away. Note the item, the date and time, where it was found, and who found it. Attach a numbered tag and place it in the lost and found room.
4. Keep valuables (phones, laptops, wallets, jewelry, passports, and cash) in the locked safe at the front desk. Two staff members must count and sign for any cash.
5. When you think you have found a match, confirm it before handing it over. Ask the person to describe details you have not mentioned, such as the case color, a lock screen image, or what is inside a bag. For a guest who is still staying, also check their ID.
6. If the guest wants the item collected, ask them to sign the found-items log with the date. Take a note of who collected it.
7. If the guest cannot come to the hotel, offer to send it by courier. Get the delivery address in writing, confirm that the guest will pay the courier fee (or that a supervisor has approved waiving it), and pack the item safely. Record the tracking number in the log and send it to the guest by email.
8. If nothing is found, tell the guest, save their contact details and item description in the log, and tell them you will check again. Search once more within 2 days.
9. Keep unclaimed items for 60 days (fictional period). After that, a supervisor decides whether to donate them to a local charity or dispose of them. Never keep or use a found item for personal use.

## What to say to the guest

If the item is found:

> Good news, [Name]. We have found your [item] and it is safe with us. To make sure it goes to the right person, could you please describe it for me? Once I have confirmed, I can arrange for you to collect it at the front desk, or we can send it to you by courier. The delivery fee is PHP [amount] and I will need your address in writing.

If it is not found yet:

> Thank you for the details, [Name]. I am sorry you are missing your [item]. I have searched our lost and found and checked with housekeeping, but I could not find it yet. I have saved your description and I will look again over the next 2 days. I will email you at [email] as soon as we have news.

## When to escalate

Escalate to a supervisor when:

- The item is very valuable (for example, jewelry or a laptop) or contains important documents such as a passport.
- The guest says the item was stolen, or suspects another guest or staff member took it. Follow the [guest complaint escalation guide](guest-complaint-escalation-guide.md), and treat it as Level 3.
- Someone other than the guest asks to collect the item and cannot prove they have permission.
- The item was found but no one can confirm the owner.
- The guest asks the hotel to cover the cost of a lost item.

Never search a guest's room while they are not present unless a supervisor and a security officer are with you.
