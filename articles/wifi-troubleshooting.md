# Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

## Purpose

This article helps you guide a guest through fixing a Wi-Fi problem in their room or in the public areas of Pahuway. Most problems can be solved in a few minutes with the steps below.

## When to use this article

- A guest cannot see the hotel Wi-Fi network.
- A guest can connect but has no internet.
- The connection is very slow or keeps dropping.
- The guest cannot get past the login page.

## Steps

1. Ask what device the guest is using, where they are (room number or area), and what they see on the screen.
2. Check that the guest is choosing the right network. The guest network is named **Pahuway-Guest**. The password is the room number and the guest's last name, all lowercase, with no spaces (for example, `412santos`).
3. Ask the guest to turn Wi-Fi off and on, then reconnect. If this does not work, ask them to "forget" the network and join again.
4. If a login page does not appear, ask the guest to open a browser and go to `http://example.com`. The login page should appear.
5. Check the device limit. Each room can connect up to 4 devices at once. If the limit is reached, ask the guest to disconnect one device or remove one from the room in the system.
6. If the connection is slow, ask the guest to move closer to the door or the window, and to close other apps that use a lot of data, such as video calls or downloads.
7. Check the network status board. If other guests have reported the same problem, the fault is likely with the hotel and not the guest's device.
8. If nothing works, offer to send the maintenance team (IT support) to the room and give a time. Log the case with the room number and what you tried.
9. Follow up with the guest after the visit to confirm it is fixed.

## What to say to the guest

> Hello [Name], I am sorry you are having trouble with the Wi-Fi. Let us try to fix it together. Please connect to **Pahuway-Guest**, and use your room number followed by your last name in lowercase as the password. If you can, turn your Wi-Fi off and on and let me know what you see.

If it is not fixed:

> Thank you for trying those steps, [Name]. I will send our IT technician to your room. They should arrive within [time]. I will call you afterwards to make sure everything is working.

## When to escalate

Escalate to IT support when:

- The steps above do not work.
- Several rooms report the same problem.
- The guest needs the internet urgently for work (for example, a video meeting in the next hour). Offer the business lounge as a temporary option.

Escalate to a supervisor if the guest is asking for compensation. See the [guest complaint escalation guide](guest-complaint-escalation-guide.md).
