# Late Check-out and Early Check-in

## Purpose

This article explains how to respond when a guest wants to arrive before, or leave after, Pahuway's standard times. It helps you decide what to approve, what to charge, and what to decline.

## When to use this article

- A guest asks to check in before 3:00 PM.
- A guest asks to check out after 12:00 noon.
- A guest arrives early and their room is not ready.
- A guest asks to store their bags before or after their stay.

## Steps

1. Confirm the guest's name, room type, and the date they need the change.
2. Open the housekeeping board and check whether the room is ready (for early check-in) or whether it is needed by an arriving guest today (for late check-out).
3. Apply the rules below:
   - **Early check-in from 12:00 noon:** free if the room is ready.
   - **Early check-in before 12:00 noon:** PHP 800 (fictional amount), only if the room is ready.
   - **Late check-out until 2:00 PM:** free if no one is arriving in that room today.
   - **Late check-out until 5:00 PM:** PHP 1,000 (fictional amount), only if no one is arriving in that room today.
   - **Late check-out after 5:00 PM:** charged as a full extra night. Send to a supervisor if the guest disagrees.
4. If the room is not ready or is needed, do not promise anything. Offer free bag storage at the front desk and take a mobile number to text the guest when the room is ready.
5. Tell the guest the time and any fee before you approve it. Wait for them to agree.
6. Add the approved time and fee to the booking notes and tell housekeeping.
7. If the request is declined, explain the reason and offer another option, such as bag storage or a lounge pass for the day.

## What to say to the guest

> Hello [Name], I would be happy to help. I can offer you a late check-out at [time] for PHP [fee / no charge]. Would you like me to confirm that? If you prefer, we can also keep your bags safely at the front desk after check-out so you can enjoy the rest of your day.

If the answer is no:

> Thank you for asking, [Name]. Unfortunately, we have a guest arriving in your room today, so I cannot extend your check-out. I can store your bags free of charge until [time] and you are welcome to use our lobby lounge. I am sorry I cannot offer more.

## When to escalate

Escalate to the front desk supervisor when:

- The guest asks for a late check-out after 5:00 PM without paying.
- A guest with a documented medical or accessibility need asks for extra time.
- The guest is a repeat visitor who says they were promised a special time previously and you cannot find the note.
- The guest is upset because a room is not ready long after check-in time. See the [guest complaint escalation guide](guest-complaint-escalation-guide.md).
