# Employee and Friends & Family Discount

## Purpose

This article explains how to verify and apply the Pahuway employee discount and the friends & family discount when a guest asks about it at booking or at check-in.

## When to use this article

- A guest says they are a Pahuway employee and asks about a staff rate.
- A guest says they are booking on behalf of, or at the referral of, a Pahuway employee (friends & family).
- A guest asks what discount they qualify for before booking.

## Steps

1. Ask the guest which discount applies: **Employee rate** or **Friends & Family rate**.
2. For an **Employee rate** request:
   - Ask for the employee's full name and department, and confirm their employee ID with HR Directory (internal lookup tool, fictional) before applying anything.
   - Employee rate: 50% off the standard room rate (fictional amount), up to 2 stays per calendar year.
3. For a **Friends & Family rate** request:
   - Ask for the name of the referring Pahuway employee and confirm with HR Directory that the employee is active and has referral allowance remaining.
   - Friends & Family rate: 20% off the standard room rate (fictional amount), up to 3 stays per calendar year per referring employee.
4. Check blackout dates. Neither discount applies during peak season (fictional dates: December 20 – January 5, and Holy Week) or when the hotel is at or above 90% occupancy.
5. If eligibility is confirmed, apply the discount code in the booking system and note which employee it is tied to.
6. If you cannot confirm eligibility (no HR Directory match, allowance used up, or blackout dates apply), do not apply any discount. Explain this to the guest and offer to check with a supervisor if they want to appeal.
7. Document the discount applied (or the reason it was declined) in the booking notes.

## What to say to the guest

> Hello [Name], thank you for letting us know. I can see the Employee/Friends & Family rate you're asking about. Let me just confirm a few details before I apply it — could I get the name and department of the employee this is linked to?

If the discount cannot be applied:

> Thank you for your patience, [Name]. I wasn't able to confirm eligibility for that discount today — [brief reason, e.g. "the referral allowance for this employee has been used for this year"]. I can still offer you our standard best available rate, or I can check with a supervisor if you'd like to appeal this.

## When to escalate

Escalate to a supervisor when:

- The guest insists they qualify but HR Directory shows no match or no remaining allowance.
- The guest is a current Pahuway employee asking for a rate beyond the 2-stay limit.
- You suspect the discount is being misused (for example, the same employee referring many unrelated bookings in a short period).

Never apply a discount you could not confirm. Say you will check with a supervisor and give a time for when you will follow up.
