# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Purpose

This article explains how to handle a guest who wants to cancel a Pahuway booking or asks for money back. It tells you which refund the guest qualifies for so you can answer quickly and consistently.

## When to use this article

- A guest wants to cancel a booking.
- A guest asks for a refund after cancelling, leaving early, or being charged twice.
- A guest asks what the cancellation rules are before booking.

## Steps

1. Find the booking using the guest's confirmation number or the email on the booking. Confirm the guest's name before sharing any details.
2. Check the booking's arrival date and rate type (Flexible or Saver).
3. Work out how far ahead of the arrival date the cancellation is:
   - **Flexible rate, 3 or more days before arrival:** full refund.
   - **Flexible rate, less than 3 days before arrival:** refund of everything except the first night.
   - **Saver rate:** no refund, but the guest may change the dates once for a fee of PHP 500 (fictional amount), subject to availability.
4. Check for a special case. Full refunds are allowed when the hotel cancelled the booking, when the hotel could not provide the room booked, or when the guest was charged twice by mistake.
   -  Partial or up to 50% refund for any announced natural disaster preventing guest from traveling to the hotel.
5. Tell the guest the outcome and the amount before you process anything.
6. Cancel the booking in the reservation system and add a note with the date, reason, and refund amount.
7. If a refund is due, submit a refund request. Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 business days.
8. Send the guest a written confirmation using the [refund confirmation template](../templates/refund-confirmation.md).

## What to say to the guest

> Hello [Name], thank you for letting us know. I have cancelled your booking [confirmation number] for [dates]. Because you cancelled [number] days before arrival on our Flexible rate, you are eligible for a [full / partial] refund of PHP [amount]. It will return to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. You will receive an email confirmation shortly. Is there anything else I can help with?

## When to escalate

Escalate to a supervisor when:

- The guest asks for a refund that the policy does not allow (for example, a Saver rate booking) and gives a serious reason such as illness or a death in the family.
- The refund amount is more than PHP 20,000 (fictional amount).
- The guest says they were charged incorrectly and you cannot see the error.
- The guest becomes upset and the conversation is not calming down. See the [guest complaint escalation guide](guest-complaint-escalation-guide.md).

Never promise an exception yourself. Say you will ask a supervisor and give a time for when you will reply.
