# Noise Complaint Handling

## Purpose

This article explains how to respond when a guest reports noise from another room, the hallway, or outside. It aims to help the complaining guest rest while treating the other guests fairly.

## When to use this article

- A guest calls or visits the desk to say a neighboring room is too loud.
- A guest reports noise from the hallway, pool area, or construction.
- Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM, and a guest reports a party or loud group.

## Steps

1. Thank the guest for reporting the problem and apologize for the disturbance.
2. Get the details: their room number, where the noise seems to come from, what kind of noise it is, and how long it has been happening.
3. Check the log to see if there are earlier noise reports for the same rooms tonight.
4. Send a staff member or the night security officer to the source of the noise. Do not ask the complaining guest to speak to the noisy guests.
5. The staff member politely reminds the noisy guests about quiet hours. This is a **first warning**. Note the time and the room.
6. Call back the complaining guest within 15 minutes to confirm what was done. Ask whether the noise has stopped.
7. If the noise continues, send staff again. This is a **second warning**. Tell the noisy guests that a third report may lead to a stay being ended without a refund.
8. If the complaining guest still cannot sleep, offer a room change if one is available. Check the housekeeping board for a quiet room on a different floor.
9. Log everything: times, rooms, warnings given, and the outcome.
10. The next day, follow up with the complaining guest using the [follow-up template](../templates/follow-up-after-resolved-complaint.md).

## What to say to the guest

> Thank you for telling us, [Name], and I am sorry your rest has been disturbed. I am sending someone to speak with the guests right now. I will call your room in about 15 minutes to check that it is quieter. If it is not, I can also offer to move you to a quieter room.

## When to escalate

Escalate to the duty manager when:

- The noise continues after a second warning.
- Guests are aggressive, threatening, or refuse to cooperate.
- You suspect illegal activity or anyone feels unsafe. Call the security desk on 000-0000 (fake number) first.
- The complaining guest asks for compensation. See the [guest complaint escalation guide](guest-complaint-escalation-guide.md).

Never share the noisy guests' names or room numbers with the person who complained.
